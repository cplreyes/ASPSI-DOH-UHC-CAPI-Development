About this platform

capi.asiansocial.org is the working home of ASPSI's computer-assisted personal interviewing projects — one place for the guides, the instruments, the live monitoring and the data.

Who runs it

Asian Social Project Services, Inc. (ASPSI) is a Philippine social-research organisation. This platform hosts the survey systems ASPSI builds and operates for its partners; the UHC Survey Year 2 is conducted for the Department of Health.

What is public, what is not

Guides, instrument documentation and this description are open — field staff and respondents need them without a password. Live monitoring and the data room hold survey responses and are behind a login held by the survey team. No respondent name or address is published anywhere on this site.

Contact

For access, corrections or questions about a survey, contact the ASPSI survey team through asiansocial.org.
