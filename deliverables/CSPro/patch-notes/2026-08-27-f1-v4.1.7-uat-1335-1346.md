## Root cause

Same three classes as v4.1.6, in two more locales/sections:
- **Held stems** (Aug-21 import could not bound the span): CEB Q44/Q45 (definition printed between the English and the Cebuano — the ILO #1326/#1327 class); ILO Q109–Q121 (shared stem on one line, per-item bracket on the next — the Q54–Q61 class; Q112/Q115/Q117/Q120 additionally carried pre-wave spans with the English item glued in); ILO Q129 (bracket wrapped over three lines), Q134 (stray `)` on the English line), Q143 (stem repeated glued to the read-aloud directive).
- **Numeric companions with their own label**: CEB Q49/Q50, ILO Q107 (`… — No. of Days`).
- **Note layer**: the shared directive `const:_READ_ONE` had no Cebuano value (an earlier hold from the inline-directive extraction defect); the Q44/Q45/Q52 definitions/preambles are hand-held `INSTRUCTIONS` rows in `generate_qsf.py` that had never been registered as notes, so no locale could carry them.

Fix = 36 label entries in `aug21-overrides.json` (F1; four of them on keys that already carried an Ilocano text, hence the new locale-keyed `keep`), applied with `apply_aug21.py --only F1 --extract <task-48 check> --fail-on-pre --apply` (CEB 4+2+1 written, ILO 31; replay 0/0/0; duplicate-label gate clean) + 4 note entries (`note:const:_READ_ONE:CEB`, `note:def:44|45|52:CEB`) rebuilt into `notes.json` with `extract_notes.py --provenance aug21` (diff = exactly those four cells + English registration of the `def:` rows in F1/F3/F4).

**Tooling changes (generator-side, tested):**
- `aug21-overrides.json`: `"keep"` may be **locale-keyed** (`{"ilo": …, "ceb": …}`) — one key, its own reviewer text per locale (+3 tests).
- `extract_notes.py`: the per-question `INSTRUCTIONS` rows (definitions/preambles, optionally `+ _CONST`) are registered as `def:<q>` notes — reviewer-supplied only, never auto-extracted (the auto-extract grabbed question stems in four locales when tried); a keep-TEXT note override now writes even when the extractor found no candidate (+2 tests).
