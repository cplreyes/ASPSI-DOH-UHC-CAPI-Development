### The rule (`anchor_extract.truncated_tail`, repo code, TDD'd)

`qa_flags()` gains `truncated-tail`, the mirror image of the June-5 `starts-mid-english`
flag. It fires when

* the value's LAST token is a **proclitic** — a case marker, article, linker or
  preposition that cannot end a phrase in any of the seven languages (`sa ang ng ki kay
  sang han ha ti iti ken nga o a …`) — and the value does not end on a terminal stop; or
* the value ends on a **lone capital letter** (the paper's English definition block
  restarted and only its article came through).

Both tests are guarded by the anchor's own English, so `Not yet, but I'm planning **to**`
and `Vitamin **A**` are not evidence of a cut. **Enclitics are deliberately excluded**
(`na` "already", `ka`, `ba`, `kadi`): `Retiro na` is a complete Hiligaynon translation of
"Retired", and holding a good value costs coverage.

The wave's defect sweep calls the same function, as the net under the flag: its three
length heuristics all require `len(en) > 40`, and `YAKAP/Konsulta or primary care
provider` is 39 characters — which is why the class shipped.

## The gate change this needed

Writing a value that equals an English label is exactly what `scan_poisoned_keys.py` calls
`SELF_ECHO`, so the write grew the reason by 6 (and `IS_OTHER_EN` by 1 for Q36, whose English
carries a stray space) and `run_aug21_gates.ps1` gate 1 printed `GATES FAILED`. That is the
correct default: an untranslated value usually means a missing translation. It is wrong for a
**proper noun the paper prints untranslated**, and the tool had no way to say so.

`data/translations-official/scan_waivers.json` is that way. Per instrument, per locale, per key,
per reason, **pinned to the value**, with a citation. Its validator refuses any reason other than
`SELF_ECHO` / `IS_OTHER_EN`, so it can never silence `DOUBLED`, `EN_FRAGMENT`,
`WRONG_Q_CLEARED`, `GLUED_CLEARED` or `STALE_KEY`. Waived rows are printed on every scan and
echoed by the gate script; a waiver that covers nothing prints `STALE WAIVER`. Covered by
`data/translations-official/test_scan_waivers.py` (21 tests) and
`F3/test_aug21_labels.py::test_each_written_ceb_source_row_is_covered_by_a_value_pinned_scan_waiver`.
