## Root cause

Three classes, all in the Aug-21 import: (1) the paper prints one question that CAPI splits into two boxes (Q5, Q18, Q58) or a
battery stem + item (Q47), so no span existed; (2) the Ilocano paper brackets its text after the English and wraps it over lines,
so the span rules cut, held or leaked it (intro B, Q4, Q14, Q15 rows, Q33, Q50, Q53, Q59); (3) the section intros, notes-to-enumerator,
definitions and the Cebuano directives are `notes.json` cells that had never been extracted for CEB/ILO — and two instruction rows
(Q17, Q34: `_CONST + "…"`) plus the by-name rows (Q18 bracket note) were never registered as notes at all.

Fix = 68 map keys + 24 note keys in `aug21-overrides.json` (locale-keyed keeps, verbatim from `text-aug21/F3_{CEB,ILO}.txt`),
`extract_notes.py` registers the two missing row shapes (`def:17`, `def:34`, `def:Q18_INCOME_BRACKET`), `notes.json` rebuilt,
`apply_aug21.py --only F3 … --apply`: CEB 26 / ILO 37 written (+ Q4 in FIL/BCL/BIS/WAR), replay 0/0/0, duplicate-label gate clean.
The poisoned-key scan's DOUBLED heuristic now checks a repeated run against the paper text before raising it (three rows are the
translator's own reduplication — *panawen, panawen*; *kangrunaan a kangrunaan*).
