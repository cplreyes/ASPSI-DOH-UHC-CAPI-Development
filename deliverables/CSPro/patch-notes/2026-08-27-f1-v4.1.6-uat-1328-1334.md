## Root cause

All seven are Aug-21 import holds or pre-wave fragments on Ilocano labels — no logic involved:
- `item:Q49_TRANCHE_INTERVAL_NUM` / `item:Q50_ACCRED_WAIT_NUM` — the numeric "No. of Days" companions carry their own label (`49. … facility? — No. of Days`), so the accepted stem translation never reached them.
- `item:Q53_WHY_DIFF_PREVENTIVE` — pre-wave map held a truncated June span with the English item glued in; `Q54`–`Q61` — the paper prints the shared stem *(Apay a narigat ti agtungpal kadagiti:)* on one line and the per-item bracket on the next, so the extractor could not bound the span (`not-in-paper`). Q59's paper uses its own stem variant *(… kadagiti sumaganad?)*.
- `val:Q62_ENROLL_RESPONSIBILITY_VS1:02` — held in **all seven** locales since the first Aug-21 extract own-matched the cover title and emitted `Head`; the corrected task-48 extract carries the real row text, so the hold was stale and every map still showed the pre-wave `Month, Day, Year`. `:05` carried a spill fragment in every locale (`ken/wenno)`, `Dashboard lamang`, `ug/o`, …). `:90` was the misspelling `San a amo` in map **and** extract (the extract anchored an unbracketed occurrence).
- `val:Q65_ENROLL_CHALL_LIST_VS1:02` — the paper prints a stray ☐ glyph inside the Ilocano bracket, which the box gate refused; `:03` carried the paper's trailing ` .`.
- `item:Q75_IS_1700_ENOUGH` — 448-char paragraph, held as `label-condensed`/overlong; the dcf label *is* the CAPI question text (generate_qsf reads the translated labels, `write_dcf` caps at 255), and the English label is itself a generator condensation.
- `item:Q98_GAMOT_FACTORS` — the paper's English stem has the typo *factoros*, so the anchor never matched; `:03` had a `b(` prefix from a line break.

Fix = 31 entries in `data/translations-official/aug21-overrides.json` (F1 block, ILO-scoped except the Q62 rows), applied with `apply_aug21.py --only F1 --extract <task-48 check> --fail-on-pre --apply` (ILO 28+2 written, 1 replaced, 1 removed; the five other locales 1 replaced + 1 removed each; HIL 2 removed), strict replay 0/0/0, duplicate-label gate clean.

**Tooling change (generator-side, tested):** `aug21-overrides.json` entries may now carry `"force": true` — the `keep` text is written even when the key is neither flagged nor absent (extract and map both hold a wrong value). Needed for `Q62:90` and `Q65:03`; validated (`force` needs keep text, never with `remove`), three tests added to `test_apply_aug21.py`. Plain `keep` text on such a key still means "hold current", as before.
