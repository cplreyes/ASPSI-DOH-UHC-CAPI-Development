## Root cause

Aug-21 ILO extractor holds on question stems whose paper layout the reader could not bound:
- `item:Q39_YK_SINCE_MONTH` / `_YEAR` — `not-in-paper` (the paper prints the stem `(No wen, manipud kaano?)` and the rows `(Bulan)` / `(Tawen)` broken across lines, Ilocano paper question 52, `text/F1_ILO.txt` 1928–1940); the pre-wave map still carried a garbled June-era span on the Month row.
- `item:Q44_CAPITATION_AMT` — the English capitation definition is printed between the English stem and the Ilocano (paper question 57, lines 2039–2046); the span began `Capitation is the amount per year …`.
- `item:Q45_PERF_INDICATORS` + `vs:Q45_PERF_INDICATORS_VS1` — the Ilocano stem is followed by the English + Ilocano definition of performance indicators (paper question 58, lines 2051–2057); the span ran on, and the pre-wave `vs:` value had a leading `(`.

Fix = five locale-scoped `keep` overrides in `data/translations-official/aug21-overrides.json` (F1 block): stem + row for Q39 (the Q5/Q6 shape), stem only for Q44/Q45 (the definitions are note-layer text) → `apply_aug21.py --only F1 --extract <task-48 check> --fail-on-pre --apply` wrote 5 ILO keys, strict replay 0/0/0, duplicate-label gate clean. `.dcf` diff vs 4.1.4 = exactly those five labels.

Cross-locale note: `item:Q44_CAPITATION_AMT` and `item:Q45_PERF_INDICATORS` have **no** value in FIL/BCL/BIS/CEB/WAR/HIL either (same hold class). Follow-up: one batch release for the six, from each locale's `text/F1_<LOC>.txt` span.
