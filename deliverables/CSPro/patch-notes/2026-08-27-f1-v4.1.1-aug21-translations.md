### The locale dimension on `aug21-overrides.json`

`apply_aug21.merge_locale()` used to look an override up as `overrides["F1"][key]`, with no
locale dimension, so an entry either governed all seven maps or none. That is right when the
defect is in the KEY (a value-set offset the English label shares) and wrong when the defect
is in ONE paper: holding such a key for all seven also suppresses the correct writes in the
other six. An entry may now carry an optional `"locales": [...]` list; without it an entry
still governs every locale, so every row written before this change keeps its meaning
(`test_apply_aug21.py`, five new tests).

### Write-set defect sweep (all 2,548 values the merge wrote)

The `english-furniture` detector was rebuilt for this sweep. Its previous form was four
literal phrases plus `AE.has_directive`, which structurally could not see either of the two
classes that reached the maps in the first cut, so its "0" was a detector artefact and is not
quoted here as a gate. It now works from the DICTIONARY: a non-anchor (`dict:`/`level:`/
`record:`) label appearing inside a value, a short pure-ASCII value that occurs verbatim in
the English corpus outside its own label (`english-own-match`), and a run of ≥3 Title-Case
ASCII words opening a new sentence after the question's `?`/`.` (`english-heading`, which is
how a paper-only section heading shows up). Against the first cut's write set the rebuilt
detector reports the 14 rows the review named (7 × `Head`, 7 × the Q140 heading) where the old
one reported 0; against this merge it reports 0.

## v4.1.1 — the row-inheritance repair (Task 49b)

The whole-branch review of this wave found a defect class the per-task reviews structurally could
not see. An option row that silently carries a **neighbouring** row's translation is well-formed and
in the right language, so no extractor flag fires — but two codes of one value set then read
**identically on the tablet**, the enumerator cannot tell the two choices apart, and the analyst
cannot tell the two answers apart afterwards.

Two mechanisms, both read off the Aug-21 papers (`task-48-report.md` §2): the paper lays an option
grid out in two columns, so one row's span comes back empty and the whole block falls to its
neighbour; and the paper itself prints one translation against two or three English rows.

Task 48 fixed the **extractor** (it now holds such a row instead of writing it) and added a
permanent `duplicate-label` gate to `apply_aug21.py` over the map an apply WOULD leave behind — the
only place both sides of a collision are visible. That stops the class being re-introduced; it
cannot repair what is already on disk, and for the two F1 rows below the paper carries **no distinct
translation at all** — their only candidate IS the neighbour's text. The honest outcome is to leave
the row untranslated: a `remove: true` override in `aug21-overrides.json` deletes the map entry and
the English label renders. **An English option label a tester can tell apart beats a wrong one they
cannot.**
