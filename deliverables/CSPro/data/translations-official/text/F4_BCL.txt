Hello, my name is    (data collector’s name) . I work for Asian Social Project Services, Inc. (ASPSI).  I am here 
to ask you to participate in a study about the Universal Health Care (UHC) and packages of programs like Yaman

and Ambulatory Services (BUCAS) center, and Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient

Marhay na aldaw. An sakuyang ngaran iyo si __________________ (pangaran nin datacollector). Ako po 
nagtratrabaho sa Asian Social Project Services, Inc. (ASPSI). Yaon po ako para mag-imbitar saindo na magheras 
sa saro na pag-aadal manungod sa Universal Health Care (UHC) asin mga pakete nin programa siring kan 
Yaman ng Kalusugan Program (YAKAP), Zero Balance Billing (ZBB), Bagong Urgent Care and Ambulatory 
Services (BUCAS), asin Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment (GAMOT). An 
Departamento nin Kalusugan (DOH) an nagpondo kan pagaadal na ini. 
 
This study aims to generate evidence on the overall experience of the general public to support continuous 
monitoring, evaluation, and learning of the implementation of the UHC Act and its Implementing Rules and 
Regulations (IRR). The questions will cover your household profiles, awareness and use of UHC programs like 
YAKAP/Konsulta, BUCAS, GAMOT, NBB, and ZBB, PhilHealth registration, health-seeking behaviors, primary 
care usage, referral experiences, and household expenditures. 
An pagaadal na ini may katuyuhan na magtipon nin ebidensya manungod sa bilog na eksperyensya nin publiko 
tanganing suportaran an padagos na pagmonitor, ebalwasyon, asin pagkasabot sa implementasyon kan UHC Act 
asin sa mga Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) kaini. An kahapotan na ini ay manungod sa household 
profile, awareness asin paggamit kan mga programa kan YAKAP/Konsulta, BUCAS, GAMOT, NBB asin ZBB, 
rehistro sa Philhealth, ugali, pagaataman, experiences sa mga referrals asin mga household expenditures. 
 
Would you like to participate as a respondent in the study? The interview may last for more or less than an hour. 
Gusto po ba nindo magpartesipar bilang respondente sa pagaadal na ini? An interbyu puede abot nin hasta 
saro may kabangang oras. 
 
We are committed to protecting your privacy. If you choose to participate, we will never share your family’s or 
child’s personal information outside of the study team. We will never include your name in information shared 
with the government or in any reports. Your name will be kept separately from your answers in a private, secure 
location. For this interview, it is also important to respect other people’s privacy and not tell anyone else what we 
talked about today. With all research, there’s a small chance that someone else might get to see your data. We do 
our best to prevent that, but if it happens, we’ll let you know as soon as possible.  
Kami po ay determinado na protektaran an saindong pribadong pagpartisipar. Kun magdesisyon kamo na

ngaran itatago na hiwalay sa saindong mga simbag sa sarong pribado asin seguradong lugar. 
 
Aside from this, there are no other risks to you if you take part in this study. As a benefit of the research, the 
knowledge gained may help the government and DOH better support your healthcare needs. We shall also provide 
Php 100 as a token of appreciation for the time you’ve shared with us.
