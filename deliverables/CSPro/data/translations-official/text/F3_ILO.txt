PART I: Information about the Study (PASET I: Impormasion maipapan iti Panagadal) 
Hello, my name is    (data collector’s name) . I work for Asian Social Project Services, Inc. (ASPSI).  I am here 
to ask you to participate in a study about the Universal Health Care (UHC) and packages of programs like Yaman 
ng Kalusugan Program (YAKAP), No Balance Billing (NBB), Zero Balance Billing (ZBB), Bagong Urgent Care 
and Ambulatory Services (BUCAS) centers, and Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient 
Treatment (GAMOT). The Department of Health (DOH) funded this study. Please let me tell you more about the 
study.  
(Hello, ti naganko ket (data collector’s name) . Agtartrabahoak iti Asian Social Project Services, Inc. (ASPSI). 
Addaak ditoy tapno kiddawek kadakayo a makipaset iti panagadal maipapan iti Universal Health Care (UHC) 
ken dagiti pakete dagiti programa a kas iti Yaman ng Kalusugan Program (YAKAP), No Balance Billing (NBB), 
Zero Balance Billing (ZBB), Bagong Urgent Care and Ambulatory Services (BUCAS) centers, ken Guaranteed 
and Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment (GAMOT). Ti Department of Health (DOH) ti nangpondo 
iti daytoy a panagadal. Pangngaasiyo ta bay-anyo nga ibagak kadakayo ti ad-adu pay maipapan iti panagadal 
 
This study aims to generate evidence on the overall experience of the general public to support continuous 
monitoring, evaluation, and learning of the implementation of the UHC Act and its Implementing Rules and 
Regulations (IRR). The questions will cover your Patient Profile, awareness of UHC and PhilHealth registration, 
primary care usage, health-seeking behaviors, specific experiences during your visit, medical costs and payment 
sources, specialized programs (YAKAP/Konsulta, BUCAS, GAMOT, NBB, ZBB, and MAIFIP), access to 
medicines, and the referral experiences. 
(Panggep daytoy a panagadal ti mangpataud iti ebidensia iti pakabuklan a padas ti sapasap a publiko tapno 
suportaran ti agtultuloy a panangmonitor, panangtingiting, ken panagsursuro iti pannakaipatungpal ti UHC Act 
ken dagiti Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR)-na. Saklawen dagiti saludsod ti Patient Profile-mo, 
pannakaammo iti panagrehistro iti UHC ken PhilHealth, panagusar iti kangrunaan a panangtaripato, kababalin 
nga agsapsapul iti salun-at, espesipiko a padas bayat ti panagbisita, gastos iti medisina ken gubuayan ti 
panagbayad, espesial a programa (YAKAP/Konsulta, BUCAS, GAMOT, NBB, ZBB, ken MAIFIP), pannakagun-
od kadagiti agas, ken dagiti padas iti pannakaiturong.)
