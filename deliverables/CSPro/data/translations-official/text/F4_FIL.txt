This informed consent form is to be obtained before conducting the interview. You must read this entire

PART I: Information about the study 
 
Hello, my name is    (data collector’s name) 
. I work for Asian Social Project Services, Inc. 
(ASPSI).  I am here to ask you to participate in a study about the Universal Health Care (UHC) and 
packages of programs like Yaman ng Kalusugan Program (YAKAP), No Balance Billing(NBB), Zero 
Balance Billing (ZBB), Bagong Urgent Care and Ambulatory Services (BUCAS) center, and 
Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment (GAMOT). The Department of 
Health funded this study. Please let me tell you more about the study.  
Kamusta, ako si pangalan ng data collector. Ako ay nagtatrabaho sa Asian Social Project Services, Inc. 
(ASPSI). Narito ako upang anyayahan kang lumahok sa isang pag-aaral tungkol sa Universal Health 
Care (UHC) at mga programa nito tulad ng Yaman ng Kalusugan Program (YAKAP), Zero Balance 
Billing (ZBB), Bagong Urgent Care at Ambulatory Services (BUCAS), at Guaranteed and Accessible 
Medications for Outpatient Treatment (GAMOT). Ang Department of Health ang nagpondo sa pag-
aaral na ito. Pahintulutan ninyo akong ipaliwanag pa nang higit ang tungkol sa pag-aaral. 
 
This study aims to generate evidence on the overall experience of the general public to support 
continuous monitoring, evaluation, and learning of the implementation of the UHC Act and its 
Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). The questions for this survey cover facility/facility head 
profile, UHC implementation changes since 2019, specialized programs (YAKAP/Konsulta, 
NBB/ZBB, BUCAS, GAMOT), DOH licensing, PhilHealth accreditation, NBB/ZBB policies, referral 
systems, human resource challenges, and secondary data such as hospital census and staffing statistics. 
Layunin ng pag-aaral na ito na makalikom ng ebidensya tungkol sa pangkalahatang karanasan ng 
publiko upang suportahan ang patuloy na pagsubaybay, pagsusuri, at pagkatuto sa implementasyon ng 
UHC Law at mga Alituntunin at Regulasyong (IRR) nito. Ang mga tanong para survey na ito ay 
sumasaklaw sa profile ng pasilidad at/o pinuno ng pasilidad, mga pagbabago sa pagpapatupad ng UHC 
mula 2019, mga espesyal na programa (YAKAP/Konsulta, NBB/ZBB, BUCAS, GAMOT), 
paglilisensya ng DOH, akreditasyon ng PhilHealth, mga patakaran ng NBB/ZBB, mga sistema ng 
referral, mga hamon sa pamamahala ng mga tauhan, at mga sekondaryang datos tulad ng census ng 
ospital at mga estadistika ng kawani. 
 
Would you like to participate as a respondent in the study? The interview may last for more or less than 
an hour. 
Nais mo bang lumahok bilang respondent sa pag-aaral? Ang panayam ay maaaring tumagal ng hindi 
baba o higit sa isang oras. 
 
We are committed to protecting your privacy. If you choose to participate, we will never share your 
family’s or child’s personal information outside of the study team. We will never include your name in 
information shared with the government or in any reports. Your name will be kept separately from your 
answers in a private, secure location. For this interview, it is also important to respect other people’s 
privacy and not tell anyone else what we talked about today. With all research, there’s a small chance 
that someone else might get to see your data. We do our best to prevent that, but if it happens, we’ll let 
you know as soon as possible.  
Kami ay nakatuon sa pagprotekta sa inyong privacy. Kung pipiliin mong lumahok, hindi namin isasama 
ang inyong pangalan sa anumang impormasyong ibabahagi sa gobyerno o sa anumang ulat. Ang iyong 
pangalan ay ilalagay nang hiwalay mula sa iyong mga sagot sa isang pribado at ligtas na lokasyon. Para

services.  I will ask about what your household used or consumed. First, I will ask which items your

household used but did not buy—such as those you produced yourselves, received as gifts, or got for 
free—and you can estimate their value. Please do not include items bought for business, resale, or for 
making other products.
