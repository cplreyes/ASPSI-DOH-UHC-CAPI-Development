learning of the implementation of the UHC Act and its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR). The questions for this survey cover

GAMOT), DOH licensing, PhilHealth accreditation, NBB/ZBB policies, referral systems, human resource challenges, and secondary 
data such as hospital census and staffing statistics.
