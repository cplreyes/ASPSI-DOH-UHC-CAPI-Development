Survey data systems for evidence that decision-makers can trust.

ASPSI designs and operates computer-assisted personal interviewing (CAPI) systems for large-scale social research — from questionnaire to clean, monitored, analysis-ready data.

What we build

Every engagement covers the full field-data lifecycle, delivered as an integrated system rather than loose tools.

CAPI Instruments

Paper questionnaires become tablet applications with built-in skip logic, validation, and multi-language support — so interviews are faster and errors are caught at the doorstep, not at encoding.

Field Data Hub

A secure central server receives data synced from the field daily. Supervisors assign workloads, track completion, and relay cases even from areas with no connectivity.

Monitoring & Dashboards

Live dashboards and maps show sync status, coverage, and data quality per area and per team — problems surface in hours, not after the fieldwork ends.

Web Survey Applications

For respondents reached outside the tablet workflow: installable, offline-first web survey apps that run on any phone and sync when a connection appears.
