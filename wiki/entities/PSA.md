# PSA — Philippine Statistics Authority

The Philippine government statistical agency. For the [[1_Projects/ASPSI-DOH-CAPI-CSPro-Development/wiki/concepts/UHC Survey Year 2|UHC Survey Year 2]], PSA's role is to **endorse and validate the sampling design**, particularly the household survey component.

## Role in the Project

- **Sampling endorsement**: [[1_Projects/ASPSI-DOH-CAPI-CSPro-Development/wiki/entities/DOH-PMSMD|DOH-PMSMD]] forwards [[1_Projects/ASPSI-DOH-CAPI-CSPro-Development/wiki/entities/ASPSI|ASPSI]]'s sampling design to PSA for endorsement (Mar 2 commitment). PSA endorsement is part of the Tranche 2 deliverable bundle.
- **Sampling consultation**: PSA participated in the Mar 12, 2026 sampling meeting with DOH and ADB; recommended ~11,000 housing units for the household survey (per Mar 3 review).
- **Statistical authority**: PSA's involvement lends statistical legitimacy to the sample design — important for downstream policy use of UHC Survey results.
- **CAPI-app assessment (added 2026-05-13)**: PSA clearance also requires the **CSPro CAPI app bundled with all 7 translated versions** — PSA will not clear the survey without it. Firm internal deadline **2026-06-12**. This is a *second, later* PSA stream distinct from the sampling/manual submission, and it is the one in Carl's lane. See [[1_Projects/ASPSI-DOH-CAPI-CSPro-Development/wiki/concepts/CAPI Seven-Language Translation Build|CAPI Seven-Language Translation Build]] and (Source: [[1_Projects/ASPSI-DOH-CAPI-CSPro-Development/wiki/sources/Source - CAPI Translations PSA Deadline (Myra 2026-05-13)]]).

## Why PSA matters to Carl

PSA endorsement on the sampling design is one of the formal items needed for Tranche 2 acceptance ("PSA + Research Ethics Board clearances" per [[1_Projects/ASPSI-DOH-CAPI-CSPro-Development/wiki/sources/Source - Signed CSA Dec 15 2025|CSA]] §5 row 2). It is **part of the trigger for Carl's C-T2 payment** (PHP 117,000), which makes PSA's review one of several gating items between Carl and his next payout.
