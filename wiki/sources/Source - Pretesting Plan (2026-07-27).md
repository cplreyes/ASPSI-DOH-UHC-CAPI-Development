## What it specifies

**Objectives.** Increase validity and reliability of the tools; flag nonsensical/biasing questions;
**validate CAPI technical functionality** (skip logic explicitly named); avoid costly rework on a flawed
digital instrument; and **determine actual completion time**, called out for the self-administered HCW tool.

**Four evaluation dimensions** applied to all five respondent types (facility head, healthcare worker,
inpatient, outpatient, household head): comprehension · logic and flow · acceptability · length and adherence.

**Method — retrospective cognitive interviewing, three phases:**
1. **Observation** — respondent completes the whole survey on a tablet while the team observes the CAPI interaction
2. **Timing** — start/end times plus approximate time per section or screen, to identify "lagging" screens
3. **Debriefing** — structured, using pre-developed verbal probes

**Sites and sample.** Los Baños and Bay, Laguna (chosen for proximity/accessibility); household component in
**Barangay Bayog or Mayondon**, Los Baños. Table 1 distributes respondents by facility type — RHU
(Los Baños/Bay), LPH-Bay District Hospital, UPLB Health Service as the Level-2 public hospital.
