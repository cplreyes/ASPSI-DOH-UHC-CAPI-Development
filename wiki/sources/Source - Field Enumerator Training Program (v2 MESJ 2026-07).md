## Design

Five-day intensive: a progression **from orientation to field readiness**. Two and a half days are set aside for the four survey tools (walkthroughs + hands-on CAPI + return demonstrations), **8 hours of supervised field practice**, and a final day consolidating through assessment. Pre-work: participants receive electronic copies of a short protocol version and the questionnaires beforehand, since those are not covered extensively in-session.

**General objective:** prepare FEs with the knowledge, skills and competencies to collect quality data for the survey.

**Specific objectives (6):** describe fieldwork procedures / recruitment / eligibility / consent; master questionnaire administration; identify and recruit respondents; solicit informed consent; administer the tools **using CAPI** while building rapport and applying interviewing techniques; **check their own work for completeness and accuracy before submission**.

## Schedule (15 modules across 5 days + Day 0)

- **Day 0 (7:00 pm)** — Registration + **Pre-test** (Appendix A), to establish baseline knowledge.
- **Day 1 — Survey orientation and field coordination.** M1 Training Overview · M2 Introduction to the UHC Survey · M3 Field Team Structure and Enumerator Responsibilities · M4 General Fieldwork Procedures · M5 Respondent Recruitment and Eligibility · M6 Informed Consent Process · M7 Building Rapport with Respondents · M8 General Interviewing Techniques. Closes with a Knowledge Check. Assessment methods: interactive lecture-discussion, **role playing**, **informed consent audit**.
- **Day 2 — CAPI and the questionnaires. (Carl's day.)** M9 **CAPI Installation** — uploading the data entry applications, accessing the tablet-based questionnaires — then walkthroughs of **F1 (Facility Head)**, **F2 (Health Care Worker)**, and **Patients (in and out)**. Assessment: **mock interviews**. Objective: *"Install and operate CAPI confidently"* and *"gain technical proficiency in the CAPI system."*
- **Day 3 — Implementation and monitoring.** Walkthrough of the **Household** questionnaire (morning) · M10 Handling Nonresponse and Callbacks · M11 Checking Completed Questionnaires · M12 Field Communication and Reporting Protocol · M13 Safety and Security Protocols.
- **Day 4 — Field practice.** Depart 7:30, practice 8:00, **field debriefing 5:00**.
- **Day 5 — Quality control, safety and final briefing.** M14 Detailed Feedback from Day 4 (sharing field-practice experience) · M15 Financial and Administrative (ASPSI forms, cash-advance liquidation, token distribution) · **Final Assessment (post-test)** · Closing.

In-charge roles named: **MESilva-Javier** (opening), the **UHC Y2 Project Team**, **CReyes & RAs** (Day 2), **RAs** (knowledge checks), **Field Managers/RAs** (field practice), **Field Supervisor** (M15).

## Mechanics of the field practice

Before: Supervising RAs, assisted by the FS, coordinate with selected health facilities and the barangay to identify a focal person and arrange — (1) interview of the facility head + secondary-data collection; (2) **the link to the online healthcare survey tool** (i.e. the [[1_Projects/ASPSI-DOH-CAPI-CSPro-Development/wiki/concepts/F2 Admin Portal|F2 PWA]]); (3) recruitment of inpatients and outpatients; (4) space to conduct interviews.

During: participants work in **groups of 2**. One interviews using CAPI (identify → recruit → consent → interview), the other observes and gives feedback, then they switch. **Only 2–3 modules** of the tool are administered; **interviews capped at 30 minutes**.

| Area | Groups |
|---|---|
| Health Facility 1 | 1 and 2 |
| Health Facility 2 | 3 and 4 |
| Health Facility 3 | 5 and 6 |
| Health Facility 4 + Community | 7 and 8 |

Hour flow: **Hour 1** facility/community coordination · **Hours 2–3** recruitment + interview + feedback · **Hours 5–7** switch · **Hour 8** conduct of LLS (see [[1_Projects/ASPSI-DOH-CAPI-CSPro-Development/wiki/concepts/LSS Meeting|LSS Meeting]]). Two groups per facility split inpatient/outpatient in the morning and swap in the afternoon. FEs bound for household-survey areas are assigned to **RHU + Community**.

## Assessment

- **Pre-test / post-test** (Appendix A, 20 items) — 60% of the score. Pre-test the evening before; post-test before the closing program.
- **Daily quizzes** (5 items, end of each day) — 40%. Formative, sequenced to reinforce orientation → survey administration → technology and data quality → deployment readiness.
- **Non-competency observations:** informed consent audit, mock interviews, field practice. Observers score: (1) introduction of self and study purpose; (2) ethical compliance; (3) maintaining neutrality; (4) appropriate probing; (5) thanking the respondent.

| Combined score | Performance level |
|---|---|
| 90–100 | **Excellent** — ready for deployment |
| 80–89 | Very good — minor coaching required |
| 70–79 | Satisfactory — additional review before deployment |
| < 70 | Needs improvement — **remedial coaching and reassessment before field assignment** |

**Appendix A** is a full 20-item examination in four parts: I. Survey Orientation and Ethics (1–5) · II. Survey Administration (6–10) · **III. CAPI and Data Quality (11–15)** · IV. Professional Conduct and Field Situations (16–20). **Appendix B** holds the daily quizzes for Days 1, 2, 3 and 5 (no Day 4 quiz — that is the field-practice day).

### The CAPI items (Carl's content)

Part III and the Day 3 quiz encode the behaviours our build must actually support, and they match how the system really works:

- CAPI's purpose is **to collect and record survey data electronically**.
- Before submitting, **review the questionnaire for completeness and accuracy**.
- On a **validation error** → *"review and correct the identified item before submission"* (not ignore, not submit anyway).
- On a **synchronization failure** → *"inform the Field Supervisor and follow troubleshooting procedures"* — never delete or re-interview. Consistent with [[1_Projects/ASPSI-DOH-CAPI-CSPro-Development/wiki/concepts/CSPro Synchronization|CSPro Synchronization]] and the escalation lines in the supervisor program.

## Implications for the CAPI lane

> [!warning] Four simultaneous sites, one named CAPI trainer
> The FE training runs **concurrently in Pampanga, Laguna, Cebu and Cagayan de Oro**, yet "CReyes" is the named in-charge for CAPI installation and all questionnaire walkthroughs on Day 2. One person cannot cover four sites at once. This needs a resolution — train-the-trainer for the RAs, a recorded/asynchronous CAPI module, staggered dates, or remote delivery. Flagging as an ASPSI scheduling decision, not a Carl deliverable.

- **CAPI installation is a Day-2 training activity for 122–125 enumerators.** The "uploading the data entry applications / accessing the tablet-based questionnaires" step is exactly the CSWeb *Add Application* flow — and the **remove + re-add** caveat in [[1_Projects/ASPSI-DOH-CAPI-CSPro-Development/wiki/concepts/CSPro Synchronization|sync]] applies.
- **Sync-URL timing.** The console and sync API have moved to `capi.asiansocial.org`; training in August is the natural moment for the fleet to learn the new address, so the cutover should land **before** training rather than after.
- Carl's existing **66-slide CAPI training deck** (built 2026-07-27 for FS training) is the raw material for both Day 2s.
