# Source - Field Supervisor Training Program (MESJ 2026-07-28 FINAL)

**The FINAL five-day Field Supervisor (FS) training program**, dated 28 July 2026 — the companion to [[1_Projects/ASPSI-DOH-CAPI-CSPro-Development/wiki/sources/Source - Field Enumerator Training Program (v2 MESJ 2026-07)|the Field Enumerator program]]. Marked FINAL (the enumerator one is still "Version 2"). Filed at `raw/training-programs-2026-07/`.

**Venue: Los Baños, Laguna. Date: August 2026** — the first document to pin the training month, matching the Aug-training / Sep-rollout shape of the extension plan.

> [!important] Carl is the named CAPI trainer here too
> **"CReyes"** owns **Day 2**: Module 6 (Using CAPI) plus walkthroughs of **all four** instruments — F1 Facility Head, F2 Health Care Worker, Patients (inpatient and outpatient), and Household. Unlike the enumerator program this is a **single venue**, so it is deliverable as written.

## Objectives

**General:** prepare FSs to supervise the field implementation of the survey.

**Specific (3):**
1. **Coordinate** with LGUs, health facilities, the field manager, research associates, enumerators and other stakeholders;
2. **Supervise** field implementation of the survey tools — adherence to field protocols, **proficiency with CAPI**, readiness for deployment;
3. **Monitor** implementation — identify, document and resolve operational issues; review field outputs; implement quality-assurance measures; and **prepare and present a field deployment plan**.

## Mechanics of the field practice

Coordination is centralised: **the ASPSI central office** (typed "ASPI" in the document) arranges the focal person; the FS then coordinates implementation arrangements — facility-head interview and secondary data, **the link to the online healthcare survey tool** (F2), inpatient/outpatient recruitment, and interview space.

**Designed as a role-playing exercise:** the focal person is given **a script that includes the challenges and constraints** an FS would realistically hit during coordination — a deliberately adversarial rehearsal, not a friendly walkthrough.

| Area | Groups |
|---|---|
| Los Baños General Hospital | 1 and 2 |
| Laguna Provincial Hospital | 3 and 4 |
| University Health Service | 5 and 6 |
| Los Baños Rural Health Unit + Community | 7, 8 and 11 |
| Bay Rural Health Unit + Community | 9 and 10 |

Same hour flow as the enumerator program (Hour 1 coordination · Hours 2–3 interview + feedback · Hours 5–7 switch · Hour 8 LLS), same 2-person groups, same 30-minute cap, same inpatient/outpatient morning–afternoon swap. The named sites are the **Los Baños / Bay, Laguna** cluster already used for pretesting.

## Assessment

Pre-test / post-test **plus** informed consent audit, mock interviews, field practice and **return demonstrations**. A week before training, FSs receive soft copies of materials (short proposal version, questionnaires) to familiarise themselves. Observers score the same five behaviours as the enumerator program (introduction, ethical compliance, neutrality, probing, thanking) on a **four-point scale**:

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| 4 | **Excellent** — demonstrates competency consistently and independently |
| 3 | Satisfactory — demonstrates competency with minor errors |
| 2 | Needs improvement — demonstrates competency inconsistently |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory — unable to demonstrate competency |

> [!note] Two different assessment schemes
> The FS program uses a **4-point observational rubric**; the FE program uses a **weighted numeric score** (60% pre/post + 40% quizzes) with 90/80/70 deployment bands. Both are internally coherent, but they are not comparable — worth knowing if anyone tries to report "training scores" across both cohorts.

## Implications for the CAPI lane

- **M11 and the Data Quality Assurance Plan are our monitoring surfaces.** *"Generate performance data from CAPI"* and *"validate CAPI submissions"* are precisely what the **Sync Dashboard**, the **Map Report** and the **data room** on the CAPI console do — supervisors are being trained to a capability we have already built. The training should show the real console.
- **Reporting and escalation lines (M3)** and *"report the problem to the Field Supervisor"* (the enumerator sync-failure item) make the FS the **first-line triage** for CAPI faults — the supervisor-facing troubleshooting content matters more than generic CAPI theory.
- **Single venue + August date** makes this program deliverable as written and sets the deadline for the sync-URL cutover, the CAPI Manual, and console credentials for supervisors.
- Supervisors are trained on the **FE-facing** interface (M6) — so a supervisor-specific console/hub walkthrough is *not* in this program's Day 2 as written, even though M9–M11 assume they can monitor. Worth confirming whether the console demo belongs in Day 2 or Day 3.
